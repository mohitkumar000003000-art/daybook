# Daybook — Personal Daily Planner & Journal

> A modern, distraction-free daily planner and personal journaling website designed for intentional living, daily scheduling, and thoughtful reflection.

---

## ✨ Features Overview

### 1. 📋 Dashboard
- **Personalized Greeting**: Time-aware greeting (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) with user's name.
- **Daily Reflection Quote**: Rotating quotes on mindfulness, intentionality, and focus from Marcus Aurelius, Virginia Woolf, James Clear, etc., with manual refresh option.
- **Progress Metrics**: Today's task count, completed count, remaining count, and dynamic completion percentage.
- **Quick Action Bar**: One-click shortcuts to Add Task, Write Journal Entry, Plan Today's MITs, and Open Calendar.
- **The Rule of 3 (MITs)**: Direct visibility and completion checkboxes for today's three core outcomes.
- **Latest Reflection Preview**: Snippet of the most recent journal entry with reading time and one-click jump to the editor.

### 2. ✅ Daily To-Do List
- **Organized Views**: Tabs for *Today*, *Upcoming*, *Overdue*, *Completed*, and *All Tasks*.
- **Task Priorities**: High, Medium, and Low with distinctive color coding and contrast.
- **Rich Task Metadata**: Due dates, optional due times, categories, and expandable notes.
- **Custom Categories**: Filter tasks by Work, Personal, Study, Health, Creative, or create custom categories with custom colors.
- **Interactive Reordering**: Drag-and-drop task reordering in custom order mode.
- **Instant Search & Sorting**: Real-time filtering by keywords, due date, priority, or alphabetical order.

### 3. 📝 Personal Daily Journal
- **Distraction-Free Editor**: Clean, spacious writing environment with warm paper textures.
- **Rich Text Toolbar**: Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Headings (H2, H3), Bulleted & Numbered Lists, Blockquotes, and Divider Lines.
- **Guided Reflection Questions**: One-click insertion of thoughtful prompts:
  - *What went well today?*
  - *What did I learn today?*
  - *What could I improve tomorrow?*
  - *What am I grateful for?*
  - *Full Daily Evening Review*
- **Photo Attachments**: Upload and preview photos directly in your entry.
- **Auto-Save & Word Metrics**: Live word counter, estimated reading time, and local auto-save status.
- **Multiple Entries per Day**: Support for continuous reflections throughout the day.
- **Markdown Export**: One-click export of any journal entry as a `.md` file with frontmatter.

### 4. 📅 Calendar & Temporal Archive
- **Month & Week Views**: Switch between monthly grid view and weekly timeline columns.
- **Activity Indicators**: Visual chips for days with scheduled tasks and journal entries.
- **Interactive Day Inspector**: Clicking any day opens the inspector drawer displaying that date's tasks, completion status, journal entries, and morning intentions.
- **Direct Scheduling**: Add tasks or journal entries directly attributed to the selected date.

### 5. 🎯 Daily Planning & Reflection (Cadence)
- **The Rule of 3 (MITs)**: Define the top three outcomes that define success for the day, with quick insertion from existing tasks.
- **Morning Intention & Energy**: Set your guiding mindset, daily focus word, and starting energy level (1–5).
- **Time-Block Schedule**: Schedule focus blocks, meetings, and breaks with start/end times and categories.
- **Evening Reflection**: Record daily wins, realizations, and gratitude before sleep.
- **Unfinished Task Rollover**: One-click button to automatically reschedule all incomplete tasks to tomorrow.

### 6. 📊 Progress & Insights
- **Habit & Writing Streaks**: Current streak, longest streak, active reflection days, and total words written.
- **7-Day Task Execution Chart**: Responsive SVG bar chart displaying completed vs. planned tasks for each day.
- **14-Day Writing Consistency Trend**: Smooth SVG curve visualizing daily writing volume.
- **Category Distribution**: Visual breakdown of tasks across life domains.
- **Accomplishment History**: Recent milestone log of completed tasks.

### 7. ⚙️ Settings, Customization & Privacy
- **Google Account Sign-In**: Dedicated section to connect your Google Account with Google account chooser dialog, personalizing your profile avatar, greeting, and verified identity.
- **Google Drive Backup Snapshot**: Download full notebook backup snapshots formatted for Google Drive.
- **Warm Paper & Deep Espresso Themes**: Switch between light parchment and dark roasted espresso palettes.
- **100% Local-First Privacy**: All tasks, journal reflections, and schedules remain strictly inside your browser's local storage. Zero tracking and zero cloud surveillance.
- **Full Data Backup (JSON)**: Export your entire notebook snapshot at any time.
- **Backup Restoration**: Upload a previously exported JSON backup to restore your data.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Built-in reference for faster navigation.

---

## 🚀 Running Locally

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start local development server
npm run dev
```

Open `http://localhost:5173/` in your browser.
